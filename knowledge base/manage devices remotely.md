## TL;DR

The easiest path is to use IP KVMs or VNC applications like [TigerVNC] or [RealVNC].\
There are also remote desktop access solutions like [TeamViewer], [MeshCentral], and [RustDesk]/[AnyDesk].

> [!important]
> [TeamViewer] took part in anti-consumer practices like removing their life license and requiring users to subscribe.
